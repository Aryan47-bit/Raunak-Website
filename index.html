About The Festival

What is रौनक (Raunak)?

Raunak, MSIT's annual Diwali cultural fest and fundraiser, not only focuses on celebrating Diwali with loved ones on campus, but also gives donations to NGOs and social welfare organizations to make their festival a little brighter.

This year, on 31st October 2026, the iconic MSIT Stairway transforms into a royal red-and-gold arena — bringing together soulful Open Mics, electrifying Live Bands, a buzzing Diwali Flea Market, gourmet Food Stalls, and an unforgettable DJ & Dance session!
